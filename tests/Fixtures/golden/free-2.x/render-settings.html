You might also like

  • Baking sourdough bread at home

    A sourdough starter needs flour, water and patience. Feed the starter daily and bake the bread when it doubles. A hot oven and steam give the loaf a crisp crust.

  • Pour over coffee for beginners

    Pour over coffee is slow and calm. Rinse the paper filter, bloom the coffee grounds, then pour hot water in circles. Light roast beans shine here.

  • Cold brew coffee in the fridge

    Cold brew coffee steeps coarse grounds in cold water overnight. Strain it in the morning for a smooth, low acid coffee concentrate.
